# License

Trellis is source-available under a three-tier license. This page summarizes it. The license files in the repository (`LICENSE.md`, `LICENSE-NONCOMMERCIAL.md`, `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md`) are the actual terms. This summary is not legal advice.

## At a glance

| Tier                        | Who                                                                                                    | How                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Non-commercial**          | Personal, educational and other non-commercial projects.                                               | Free.                                                                                                      |
| **Commercial (individual)** | Individuals using Trellis commercially.                                                                | An active [GitHub Sponsorship](https://github.com/sponsors/danfessler) at or above the **$10/month** tier. |
| **Commercial (studio)**     | Organizations with **10 or fewer** members.                                                            | An active GitHub Sponsorship at or above the **$100/month** tier (a studio-wide license).                  |
| **Enterprise**              | Organizations with **more than 10** members, or anyone needing priority support, custom terms or SLAs. | Contact [dan@danfessler.com](mailto:dan@danfessler.com).                                                   |

## Non-commercial use

You may use, modify and share Trellis for personal, educational and non-commercial projects, as long as you keep the license file and include attribution in distributed versions.

Non-commercial means no commercial purpose, directly or indirectly. It excludes, for example, products or internal tools that generate revenue, work done within a for-profit organization (including side projects done as part of employment), and client work. Forks and derivative works are allowed for non-commercial purposes under the same terms.

## Commercial use

While you're an active sponsor at the applicable tier, you may:

- Use Trellis in commercial projects.
- Modify it and integrate it into proprietary software.
- Use it for internal business purposes and client work.
- Bundle it with software you distribute.

If your product or service is publicly accessible, include reasonable attribution in an About page, documentation, footer, repository or README. The attribution reads:

> “Uses Trellis by DanFessler — github.com/DanFessler/trellis”

You may not resell Trellis on its own, re-license or sub-license it (except within your products), or share the source in a way that circumvents the licensing model. If your sponsorship ends, your commercial rights end. You then need to stop commercial use, renew, or arrange another license.

## Enterprise

Organizations with more than ten members, or that need priority support, custom licensing terms, legal review or SLAs, need an enterprise license. Email [dan@danfessler.com](mailto:dan@danfessler.com).
